Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Blue Light Filter does not collect, store, send, or sell any personal data.

  • The extension saves only your filter settings (on/off, strength, warmth, and schedule times). These are stored on your own computer using Chrome's built-in storage and never leave your device.
  • It does not read, record, or send the content of the web pages you visit. It only places a see-through colored layer on top of them.
  • It does not use analytics, ads, trackers, cookies, or any outside servers.

This website also does not use cookies, analytics, or trackers.
