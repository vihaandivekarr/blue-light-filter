Easier on your eyes at night

Blue Light Filter is a free Chrome extension that puts a warm, see-through color over web pages so your eyes get less blue light, with different settings for each site.

Add to Chrome, it's free

No account. No ads. No tracking.

New extension: Chrome may say it's "not trusted by Enhanced Safe Browsing." That message appears for every new developer until they build a track record, and it isn't about anything the extension does. Learn more.

Features

Settings for each site

Make one site extra warm, dim a bright shopping site, or turn the filter off on Google Docs.

Never breaks websites

No color inverting or restyling. Text, images, and layouts stay exactly as they are.

Warmth, strength, and dim

Pick any warmth from 6,500K to 1,000K, set the strength, and dim bright pages.

Presets

One click for Daylight, Evening, Night, or Bedtime.

Night schedule with fade

Turns on by itself at the times you choose and can fade in gradually.

Works in fullscreen

Fullscreen videos stay warm too.

Shortcuts and pause

Alt+Shift+B to toggle, Alt+Shift+Up/Down for strength, or pause for an hour.

Works right away

Starts on every open tab the moment you install it. No refreshing.

Private

No data collection, no ads, no redirects. Your settings stay on your computer.
