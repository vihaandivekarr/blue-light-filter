Easier on your eyes at night

Blue Light Filter is a free Chrome extension that puts a warm, see-through color over web pages so your eyes get less blue light.

Add to Chrome, it's free

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Features

One-click on/off

Turn the warm filter on or off from the toolbar.

Adjustable strength

Slide from a light tint to a strong one.

Three warmth levels

Pick Yellow, Amber, or Red.

Night schedule

Turns on by itself at the times you choose, like 8 PM to 7 AM.

Works everywhere

Works on every website, and links and buttons still work normally.

Private

Your settings stay on your computer. Nothing is collected.
